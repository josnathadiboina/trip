Good to Know

What if I need to cancel? ⌄
Head to Profile → Bookings, pick the trip and tap Cancel. Your refund is worked out instantly and tracked live until it reaches you.
Why do fares change over time? ⌄
Prices move with demand, season and availability. View any listing's full price history before you book.
How do I use a promo code? ⌄
Simply enter the code at payment — it's validated instantly and the discount is applied to your total.
How soon will my refund arrive? ⌄
Most refunds complete within 5 business days. Follow each stage live in the tracker on your Profile.
