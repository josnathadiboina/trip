FAQs related to Bookings

How do I cancel a booking? ▾
Go to your Profile → Bookings, select the booking and choose Cancel. Refunds are calculated automatically based on our cancellation policy.
How does dynamic pricing work? ▾
Prices adjust in real time based on demand, seasonality and how many seats or rooms are left. You can view the full price history on any listing.
Can I apply a coupon at checkout? ▾
Yes — enter your coupon code on the payment step. It will be validated instantly and the discount applied to your total.
How long do refunds take? ▾
Refunds move through Pending → Processed → Completed, typically within 5 business days. Track live status from your Profile.
